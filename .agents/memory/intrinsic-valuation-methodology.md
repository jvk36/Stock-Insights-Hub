Use trailing-twelve-month EPS, not a single quarter's EPS, and a three-year EPS CAGR for Intrinsic Valuation.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected TTM and three years after clarification that annual P/E multiples applied to unannualized quarterly EPS would materially understate values.

The agreed latest-point interpretation averages reported TTM EPS with projected TTM EPS incorporating the next quarter's consensus, rather than averaging annual and quarterly numbers. Keep CAGR based on reported earnings; do not introduce forecast growth into historical points.

**Why:** This keeps the confirmed annual earnings basis consistent while preserving the requested latest-point blend.

**How to apply:** Preserve the piecewise multiple rule, including uncapped multiples above 15% growth. Describe the curve as an earnings-based reference, not direct cash-flow valuation or guaranteed price reversion. Historical illustrations are retrospective and may use restated facts; do not represent them as point-in-time backtests. Missing or unreconciled currency/share units must produce an explicit unavailable value, never a guessed USD valuation.
