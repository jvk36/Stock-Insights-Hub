# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public stock research pages, including `/macro`, `/13f`, `/13f/:slug`, and `/pricing`.
- Public crawlability, social sharing, AI-crawler visibility, and source-detectable WCAG 2.2 A/AA accessibility.

## Out of scope
- Authenticated account, admin, watchlist, stock detail, and premium stock-screen pages.
- API-only routes and the internal mockup sandbox, except where they control public-page delivery.

## Target audience
- Self-directed investors researching macroeconomic indicators, public companies, and institutional 13F portfolios.

## Primary keywords
- Stock research platform
- Macroeconomic indicators
- Hedge fund 13F holdings and insights
- Premium stock research

## Crawler assumptions
- Public pages should be indexable and shareable.
- Social preview and AI crawlers generally do not execute client-side JavaScript.
